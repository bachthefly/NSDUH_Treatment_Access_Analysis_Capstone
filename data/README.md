# Data

This directory contains the datasets and supporting data documentation used in the analysis.

## Source

**2023 National Survey on Drug Use and Health (NSDUH)**  
Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA)
An agency of the United States Department of Health and Human Services

The NSDUH is a nationally representative annual survey of the U.S. civilian, noninstitutionalized population.

## Data Used

The analysis focuses on adults aged 18 and older and examines two treatment-access outcomes:

- `MHTRTPY` — Past-year mental health treatment
- `SUTRTPY` — Past-year substance use treatment

## Data Availability

The original NSDUH public-use data are not included in this repository. Users should obtain the dataset directly from SAMHSA.

See the main README for the analytical methodology and preprocessing decisions.
